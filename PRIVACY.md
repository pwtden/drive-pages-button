# Drive Pages Button: privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026.

Drive Pages Button collects no personal data, and no other data either. It sends nothing to its publisher or to anyone else.

## What it reads

- **The address of the tab in front of you**, and only when you click its toolbar button. This is Chrome's `activeTab` permission. The extension uses the address to tell whether a Google Drive folder or file is open, and to put that folder's or file's id into the addresses of the buttons. It does not read the page's content.
- **Its own configuration**, from Chrome's storage. This is either what your organisation's administrator set through Chrome policy, or what you saved on its options page, which Chrome may sync to your other browsers signed in to the same Google Account.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect, store, sell or transfer any data about you, your browsing or your files.
- It does not call any service, use analytics, or load code from anywhere else.
- It does not read any other tab, any page's content, your cookies or your history.

## The pages it opens

Each button opens an address chosen by your organisation, or by you. Those pages are not part of this extension: they have their own sign-in and their own privacy terms.
